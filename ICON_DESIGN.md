# 声笺图标

使用内置 imagegen 工具生成，复制到项目并以 sips 规范为 1024×1024、不透明 PNG。完整原图保留在本机生成目录。Xcode 使用单尺寸 AppIcon 资产生成 iPhone/iPad 所需尺寸；外轮廓由系统裁切。

资源：`SenseVoiceApp/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`。
资料库页签使用有效的系统符号 `folder.fill`；录音页签使用 `mic.fill`。

## 最终生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production iOS home-screen app icon, 1024 by 1024 square opaque PNG. Primary request: an elegant minimal icon for 声笺, a private voice journal app that records speech and saves written notes. Background: vivid warm orange matching the app's orange interface, subtle smooth orange gradient covering every edge of the square. Subject: a bold white central mark combining an upright microphone with a small folded note/page and three simple sound-wave strokes. Clean geometric forms, optically balanced, very clear at 60px. Flat polished Apple-platform app identity. Generous safe margin around the white mark. No text, no letters, no border, no surrounding device mockup, no pre-rounded outer corners, no transparent pixels, no shadow outside the canvas. One icon only.
```
